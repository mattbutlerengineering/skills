# Launch: An animated factory floor for every piece of work in your repo

## What it is

The assembly line turns all of your repo's work into one animated factory floor you can open in a browser. Every piece of work is a crate that sits at the station for the step it is on.

## What it does

It writes a single page that shows each piece of work moving along the line, with the finished ones stacked on shelves. You can hover a crate for its details and replay the repo's history to watch the line fill up over time.

## How it helps

You see at a glance where work is piling up and what has been finished. It is also something you can show other people without explaining it.
