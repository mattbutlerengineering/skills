How to read this

  • A crate is one run: a feature or fix in flight.
  • Belts carry crates through the stages, in the arrows' direction.
  • The amber sign marks the busiest station — the pile-up.
  • Implement crates carry their work orders as parts; click one to see them.
  • The city at the end of the line is finished work: one named building per shipped run.
  • Tape colour is the run's kind: green feature, amber fix, purple product.

Hover anything for detail; click to pin a list. Press Play to replay the repo's whole history.
