How to read this

  • A crate is one run in flight: feature, maintenance or product.
  • Belts carry crates through the stages, in the arrows' direction.
  • The amber sign marks the busiest station — the pile-up.
  • Implement crates carry their work orders as parts — green done, grey open; clicktap one to see them.
  • The city at the end of the line is finished work: one named building per shipped run.
  • Tape colour is the run's kind: green feature, amber maintenance (taped across), purple product.
  • The bin holds runs the placement tool could not read; hovertap one for the reason.
  • The ticks over the slider are each day's arrivals — taller is busier; hover the slider for a day's date and count.drag it to that day.
